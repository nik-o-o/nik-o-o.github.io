Hearing the Feeling

Can a model listen to a sales call and flag the moments a customer gets upset, so a manager only reviews what matters?

0.79
macro-F1 on speakers the model never heard. The baseline scored 0.58, chance is 0.125.
91%
recall on angry clips
8%

  • Eight-emotion classifier on RAVDESS speech: 1,440 clips from 24 actors, always tested on unseen speakers.
  • Final model blends 70% frozen WavLM-large (layer 9 of 24) with 30% of a classical model on 195 hand-made acoustic features.
  • Scores become three lanes for each call moment: auto-flag, manual review, no action. 98% of auto-flags were truly negative.

Built with PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, WavLM, HuBERT, scikit-learn, librosa, Streamlit

ISB advanced statistics, team project

Winning the Next Point

Standing at 30–40 on a Tuesday, what should a coach change? We modelled the chance a server wins the next point using only what is known before the ball is hit.

326k
Wimbledon service points, 2011 to 2019
0.64
AUC, up from 0.57 after adding second-serve features
+8 pts
set-win chance for a man who wins 5 more of every 100 first-serve points
  • Strict pre-point features: score, server, form and records. Serve speed, rally length and winners happen during the point, so they stay out.
  • Grouped cross-validation, a 2019 hold-out and held-out players to check that the model transfers.
  • Gradient boosting matched logistic regression (0.643 against 0.642 AUC), so the readable model won.

Built with Python, pandas, logistic regression, gradient boosting, cross-validation

Price cuts need a sign

Should a grocer cut Coke’s price quietly, or put up a deal tag? A log-log regression on 52 weeks of scanner data from seven Dominick’s stores.

−2.81
Coke price elasticity in a normal week
−4.30
with a visible deal tag: 1.53 times as responsive
p = 0.88
Pepsi on deal had no measurable effect on Coke
  • Three models: own and cross price, then deal indicators interacted with price.
  • Advice: pair every discount with a visible tag, stagger the two brands’ promotions, and promote to grow the category instead of starting a price war.
  • Same assignment: a logistic model of loss-making orders against discount depth on 10,194 Superstore lines, with discount ceilings by category.

Built with R, R Markdown, log-log OLS, logistic regression, ggplot2

ISB supervised learning, individual

Air fares: segment before you model

300,153 domestic fares. A regression R² of 0.911 looked great until travel class turned out to carry 58% of it. Inside Economy the same model explained 3.5%. Rebuilt on Economy it reached 0.51, and a depth-9 decision tree reached 0.956 with RMSE 29.5% lower than regression. The same assignment includes an HR offer-joining classifier with 0.99 recall and 0.75 ROC AUC.

Built with scikit-learn, regression, decision trees, random forest

ISB deep learning, individual

Four deep-learning studies

Backprop by hand, churn, spam and logos, each with a control model that could prove the fancy one unnecessary. On SMS spam, a bag-of-words control beat Simple RNN, LSTM and GRU on the three-seed average, and the spread between seeds was more than 20 times the gap between the top two models. About 60 churn models trained across seeds.

Built with TensorFlow, Keras, RNN, LSTM, GRU, transfer learning

Six rules I follow now

Each one came from a project on this page.

Split by speaker, not by row

Our features identified the speaker 97% of the time. A random split let the model recognise voices instead of emotions.

A random split inflated macro-F1 by 17 points.

Hearing the Feeling

Freeze time before the serve

Rally length and serve speed predict the point beautifully because they happen during it. At decision time they do not exist yet.

Only pre-point features allowed.
